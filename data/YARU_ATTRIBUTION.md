# Yaru-derived structural colors

Surface/text/border hex values in `data/style/style.css` are taken from
Ubuntu **yaru-theme-gtk** GTK4 gresource `@define-color` tables for themes
**Yaru** (light) and **Yaru-dark**.

- Upstream: https://github.com/ubuntu/yaru  
- License: GPL-3.0 (compatible with ChickenButt GPL-3.0-or-later)

This app does not ship the full Yaru stylesheet. Accent colors come from the
system/libadwaita portal (`--accent-bg-color`, etc.), not hard-coded Yaru orange.
